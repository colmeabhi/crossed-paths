## Why this dataset

It's the only option found with **all three** encounter signals in one place:

| Dataset | Co-presence | Location | Conversation | Why not |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lancaster GoT** (chosen) | ✓ scenes | ✓ scene locations | ✓ scripts | — |
| SNAP ego-Facebook | — | — | — | friendships only, anonymous |
| SNAP Brightkite | ✓ | ✓ check-ins | — | no conversation signal; must be cut to one city |
| Beveridge GoT networks | mixed | — | mixed | "met" and "talked about" merged into one weight |
| Serial Speakers | — | — | ✓ | labels only for some episodes; text encrypted |
| Yelp Open Dataset | — | ✓ | — | multi-GB |

## Known limitations

Worth stating in the report:

- **Fictional data.** It's a stand-in for real wearable signals, not evidence about real behavior.
- **Many minor users.** Every character is a user, including one-scene extras such as "Lannister Soldier", so many users have very few connections.
- **Synthetic users and follows.** These were added to meet the size requirement; their follow patterns are random, weighted toward popular characters.
- **Bag-of-words scripts.** Names can only be matched one word at a time, so multi-word names and ambiguous first names rely on a hand-checked nickname list. Only characters with 10+ scenes are matched automatically.
- **Mentions ≠ affection.** Arya's kill list and Ramsay → "Reek" show that saying a name means someone matters, not that you like them. That's why mentions are capped.
- **Time-varying names.** "Snow" meant Jon *and* Ramsay (until Ramsay was legitimized). It was skipped entirely, losing some of Jon's mentions.
- **Locations are coarse.** They're regions ("The North"), not places ("Winterfell").
- **No per-season counts.** The season slider knows when a pair *first* met, not how close they were at that point. Mentions have no season.
- **Seeded follows are synthetic.** They're derived from shared scenes, so follows and co-presence are correlated by construction.
